# Employee Manual
## Enterprise Visitor Management System

This manual is for Employees managing their visitors.

### Your Responsibilities
- **Pre-Register Guests**: Always use the `Appointments` tab to pre-register your expected visitors. This speeds up their check-in time significantly at the Security Gate.
- **Approve Visitors**: When a walk-in visitor arrives to see you, you will receive an alert. You must `Approve` or `Reject` the visitor from your Dashboard.
- **Meeting Etiquette**: Visitors are automatically tracked for their meeting duration. Please ensure they check out at the reception or security desk to close their loop.
