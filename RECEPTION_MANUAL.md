# Reception Manual
## Enterprise Visitor Management System

This manual is for Receptionist Staff.

### Daily Operations
- **Pre-Registrations**: View all scheduled appointments for the day on the Front Desk Dashboard.
- **Walk-in Processing**: Assist walk-in visitors with registration. Direct them to the Visitor Kiosk or register them manually on their behalf.
- **Host Notification**: Monitor the dashboard to ensure the Smart Alerts engine successfully notified hosts when their visitors arrived.
- **Reporting**: End-of-day reports can be generated via the `Reports` tab to reconcile the visitor logbook.
