# User Manual
## Enterprise Visitor Management System

Welcome to the Enterprise VMS. This system is designed to seamlessly manage the flow of external visitors, contractors, and guests across the facility.

### Quick Start
1. **Access the Portal**: Open the VMS URL in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox).
2. **Select Role**: Choose your designated role (Admin, Security, Reception, Employee).
3. **Dashboard Navigation**: Use the sidebar to navigate between operational screens.
4. **Theme Preference**: Use the Sun/Moon icons in the top right to toggle between Light and Dark mode.

### Support
If you experience a `403 Access Denied` or `Unexpected Error`, please contact your IT Administrator with the timestamp and browser details available in the `/admin/health` panel.
